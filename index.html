Maker guides

Print it right the first time

01

Print the fit test

Every kit ships with a small coupon. Pins should slide into the 5.4 mm hole by hand, dowels should grip in the 3.2 mm hole. Tight? Raise your slicer's XY hole compensation by 0.1 mm.

02

Use the right settings

PLA or PETG, 0.2 mm layers, 3 walls, 15–20 % infill, no supports. Pins, clips and dowels at 100 % infill and 0.12–0.16 mm layers. Parts are pre-oriented – don't rotate them.

03

Follow the arrows

Our PDF guides show every part with a code and an orange arrow to where it goes. Moving joints stay dry – no glue, no grease. Check the function after each step.

04

Laser & CNC files

Red hairline = cut. DXF files are in millimetres. Scale freely, but check thin parts against your kerf or bit size (about 1 mm) when you go much smaller.

05

Unzip on a computer

Large bundles are split into several ZIPs only because of Etsy's file limit. Download all of them and unzip on a computer, not a phone.

06

Need help?

Message us on Etsy with a photo of the part and your slicer settings. We answer fast and help you get it built.

FAQ

Good to know

Do I get a physical product?

No – everything is an instant digital download (STL, 3MF, SVG, DXF, PDF). You print, cut or route it yourself.

Which software opens the files?

STL/3MF: Bambu Studio, PrusaSlicer, OrcaSlicer, Cura, Anycubic Slicer, Creality Print. SVG/DXF: LightBurn, xTool Creative Space, Glowforge, Cricut Design Space (SVG), Inkscape, Fusion 360.

Can I sell what I make?

Yes, finished physical items you make yourself – the exact limit is written in each listing. Sharing or reselling the digital files is not allowed.

Are the images real photos?

Product images are digital renders and mockups unless the listing says it shows a printed sample. Designs and documentation are created with AI assistance and checked by software.

Can you make a custom vehicle?

Send us an Etsy message with the vehicle, your process (3D print, laser, CNC) and the size. We'll tell you if it's feasible before you order.
